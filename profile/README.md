## What φ is

φ is a writing app, not a note-taking app you wrestle into a manuscript.
It keeps your draft, your notes, and every version together as plain files
on your own machine — no account, no cloud, and nothing to lock you in.

## What's inside

- **Focus mode** — a clean page and nothing else, with typewriter scrolling and a reading mode.
- **Long-form structure** — organize a manuscript into collections (books, parts, chapters) with per-chapter word goals and an outline.
- **Version history** — automatic checkpoints and named snapshots; restore any earlier draft with a click.
- **Links & graph** — connect notes with `[[wiki-links]]`, follow backlinks both ways, and see the whole shape of a project.
- **Writing-native blocks** — verse, scene breaks, epigraphs, pull-quotes, and callouts.
- **Annotations** — colored highlights and margin notes anchored to the text.
- **Spell-check** — as-you-type checking in several languages, with a personal dictionary.
- **Export anywhere** — Markdown, HTML, or plain text, and import whole vaults from Obsidian and Logseq.

## Local-first, and yours

Your writing lives in a folder you choose, as plain files you own. There's
nothing to sign up for and nothing is uploaded — your words stay on your
machine, alpha or not.

## Public alpha

φ is in public alpha for **macOS, Windows, and Linux**. Expect rough edges;
your work stays safe regardless, saved continuously and versioned as you go.
